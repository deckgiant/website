Custom Pavilion Builder in Denver, CO.

Deck Giant designs and builds custom cedar, gable-roof pavilions and gazebos for homeowners throughout Denver, the south metro, and out into the Colorado foothills — built as a true outdoor room, not just shade.

Pavilions are one of Deck Giant's core specialties — a fully roofed, finished structure built for year-round use, not just shade. Whether you want a simple covered sitting area or a full outdoor kitchen and fireplace setup, we design and build it to fit your property and your needs. We thrive in difficult terrain, steep slopes, and complicated projects — no job is too complex.

Tongue-and-Groove Ceilings

A finished wood ceiling that turns your pavilion into a true outdoor room.

Cedar pavilion with a tongue-and-groove ceiling over an outdoor lounge area

CLEAN, FINISHED LOOK

A True Ceiling, Not Exposed Framing

Tongue-and-groove boards run edge to edge across the underside of the roof, fully covering the rafters and framing above. Instead of looking up at exposed lumber, you get a smooth, finished wood ceiling — the same look and feel as an indoor room.

Cedar tongue-and-groove ceiling with a fan and recessed lighting

BUILT FOR ADD-ONS

Fans, Recessed Lighting & More

A finished ceiling gives us a clean surface to mount a ceiling fan, recessed lighting, or a speaker, with wiring run above the boards instead of hanging exposed. It's available in cedar to match your pavilion, and pairs well with an outdoor kitchen or fireplace setup for year-round use.
